Good to know

Questions, answered

Is it free?

Yes, completely. No ads and no locked features; MeppleTime is a passion project for people who love game nights.

Do my friends need accounts?

No. Voters open the link, type a first name and vote. Creating a poll needs no account either; signing in is optional and only keeps your polls together across devices.

Why not just any date poll?

Generic polls stop at dates. MeppleTime knows games have player counts, lets the group vote on what to play with BoardGameGeek search built in, and shows who is actually coming.

How long do polls stick around?

Until about a year after the last proposed date, then they are cleaned up automatically.

Does it work on phones?

Everywhere: it runs on any device with a browser, and you can install it to get an app icon on your home screen.

Is it available in Polish?

Yes: the whole app and this page speak Polish and English. The toggle is in the top corner.
