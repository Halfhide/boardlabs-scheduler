Good to know

Questions, answered

How do I schedule a board game night with friends?

Create a poll with a few possible dates, share the link in your group chat and let everyone answer yes, maybe or no. The group availability view shows the best night; lock it, and everyone sees who is coming. Setting up the poll takes a minute or two.

Is it free?

Yes, completely. No ads and no locked features; MeppleTime is a passion project for people who love game nights.

Do my friends need accounts?

No. Voters open the link, type a first name and vote. Creating a poll needs no account either; signing in is optional and only keeps your polls together across devices.

Can we vote on which game to play?

Yes. Anyone in the poll can suggest games, with live BoardGameGeek search, and vote for every game they would enjoy. The game with the most votes shows up next to the winning date. Game voting is optional.

Is MeppleTime a Doodle alternative?

For game groups, yes. The date poll works the way you know from Doodle: one link, no accounts for voters, and yes, maybe or no on each date. On top of that, MeppleTime handles what game nights need: game suggestions and votes, player counts, and a clear view of who is coming. It is free and has no ads.

Does it work on phones?

Everywhere: it runs on any device with a browser, and you can install it to get an app icon on your home screen.

Is it available in Polish?

Yes: the whole app and this page speak Polish and English. The toggle is in the top corner.

How long do polls stick around?

Until about a year after the last proposed date, then they are cleaned up automatically.

What data does MeppleTime store?

As little as possible: poll titles and dates, the names voters type in, votes, comments and game suggestions, plus your sign-in details if you choose to sign in. The app has no ads, analytics or tracking scripts; this website counts page views anonymously. The privacy page has the details.

Who makes MeppleTime?

MeppleTime is an independent side project made by one board game fan. The source code is open on GitHub under the GPL-3 license.
