How it works

1

Pick possible dates

Create a poll in seconds and share one link. Nobody needs an account or an app.

2

Everyone votes

Yes, no or maybe on each date, live for the whole group, on any device.

3

Play

Lock the winning night, pick the game together, and see exactly who is coming.

The best night jumps out

The group availability view shades every date by how many people can make it. No spreadsheet, no twenty-message thread.

Who can, who can't, who's thinking

A Doodle-style table shows everyone's answers side by side and updates live as votes come in.

Decide what you'll play, too

Anyone can suggest games, with live BoardGameGeek search, and vote for favorites. The leading game shows up next to the winning date.
